# Field Notes

A field guide to the wildlife of ordinary places — photography and observations
from Tampa and Pasco County, Florida.

Most of the wildlife people actually meet isn't in a preserve. It's on a church
lawn, in a retention pond behind a parking lot, in the grass outside the back
door. This is a guide to that.

**Live site:** https://nicofoxheart.github.io/Nicolas-Graham-Nature/

---

## Contents

- **Six ordinary places** — a map of where the photographs were taken.
  Locations on private property are shown approximately on purpose.
- **Seven residents** — the animals that live in them.
- **The aquarium** — because almost nobody reading this will ever dive a reef,
  and that counts as an ordinary place too.

Identifications that aren't certain are labelled as such. Uncertainty is part of
a field guide; it just has to be marked.
